# Delivered edit

Five existing product-film chapters take the room from a silent tap to a delivered line. The edit then switches to the working projector. This master uses the earlier five-beat choreography and the earlier 62-second editorial timeline as its starting point.

| Time | Picture | Sound or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | A single dark silhouette gives way to two classic earbuds. “Say it without saying it.” | A low musical bed establishes space and builds with the light. |
| 8–18 s | The coach’s tap crosses to the presenter. “Slow down.” A receipt travels back. | Tap, arrival cue, stock synthetic whisper, receipt. |
| 18–28 s | Four cached lines fan out between the seats. | Four separately timed signals follow the graphic. |
| 28–38 s | The question moves privately to the coach. Approval releases the whisper to the presenter. | Draft cue, approval and the delivered line. |
| 38–46 s | The illustrated timeline reconstructs the conversation. | Individual events receive a short musical accent. |
| 46–54 s | The real Live board shows a confirmed whisper. Reveal shows the messages, counts and timeline recorded in that session. | Two browser seats completed the rehearsal through the original hub. The on-screen totals come from that take. |
| 54–62 s | Telepathy wordmark and end card. | Final pulse, chord release, silence. |

The source is in edit/timeline.json, edit/comp and edit/audio. The renderer uses local studio lights, the classic GLB, a fixed capture resolution and a bounded image cache. The final 3D frames and live-stage captures are build outputs.
