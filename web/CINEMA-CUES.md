# Cinema run of show

A presents. B coaches from the audience. The operator runs the projector. The room should understand one thing by the end of the pitch. B can help A change course without taking the floor.

## Before the audience arrives

Use the Wasp cinema connected to the room’s original hub for a physical demo. The desktop package and its two simulated phones are for local rehearsal. Have each person complete voice setup, test the output in their ear and leave the phone awake. Select Pitch. If the live question beat will use the room, check microphone consent, the room transcript and any references first.

The operator sends Sound check and waits for both seats’ playback confirmations and a nod from each person. Start the session in the stage, then project the cinema’s opening title. Space plays one chapter and holds the image. Speak at the room’s pace.

| Cue on the projector | What A and B do | What the operator does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Say it without saying it.** Two classic earbuds emerge in separate pools of light. | A opens with “What if your teammate could steer your pitch without interrupting it?” B watches from the audience. | Hold the two-earbud frame through the opening line. |
| **One tap. Your voice, in their ear.** A blue signal crosses from coach to presenter. | A speeds up slightly. B uses **Single** on the phone’s Live screen to send “Slow down.” A changes pace after hearing it and uses **Single** within the receipt window. B listens for the receipt. | Advance on A’s transition. Leave the frame up while the exchange happens. |
| **Four messages, ready before you begin.** The four paths open between the earbuds. | B uses **Double** for “One minute left.” A continues the pitch. The other fixed lines can stay in reserve until needed. | Hold on the complete fan so the audience can read the lines. |
| **The AI drafts. You decide.** The coach hears the illustrated draft before it crosses to A. | In the simulated take, B enters “The judge on the left looks lost.” B hears the preview, chooses **Send**, and A hears the approved line. For a live question, A can request private coaching and answer aloud in their own words. | Advance at the explanation of approval. Keep the discussion on the path the room is actually demonstrating. The two-seat approval and a private question are separate actions. |
| **It was on the whole time.** The paths settle into a timeline. | B uses **Out** to send “Wrap it up now.” A closes the pitch after hearing it. | After the last chapter, press Space to open the stage. Check that the closing message shows playback, then choose **Reveal**. Read this session’s counts and point to its actual messages. |

## If the room takes a different path

The Live phone controls have the same named signals when Bluetooth callbacks are unavailable. If the stage reports a seat offline, keep its phone page open. If the phone asks for setup, choose the same seat, finish the prompts and let its cache become ready before sending the next cue. If live question answering is unavailable, finish the fixed exchange and show its actual Reveal. For a separate demonstration of the approval sequence, open **Operator → Canned take** and identify it as the bundled rehearsal.
