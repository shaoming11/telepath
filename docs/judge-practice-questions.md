# Judge practice sheet

For the friend playing a judge in the Pitch Q&A test. Ask the questions out loud, near the MacBook. Pause after each question and let the presenter answer before moving on.

The presenter uses the pitch scenario. The coach answers from the project docs, including the [pitch brief](telepathy-pitch-brief.md) and the [pitch cue sheet](pitch.md). Upload the [pitch and video outline](pitch-and-video-outline.md) as well, so cues match the planned pitch.

## How to run it

1. Ask one question at a time, in a normal voice.
2. Wait. The presenter should hear a cue within a few seconds, then answer in their own words.
3. Note what the presenter heard and whether it helped. The stage lists every cue under the answer panel.
4. Mix in follow-ups. Real judges dig into the first answer.

## Warm-up questions

The docs cover these well, so every one should produce a useful cue.

- What problem are you solving?
- Walk me through how it works.
- Why does it use the MacBook microphone instead of the phone?
- How did you build it?
- What does ElevenLabs do in your system?
- What does Maritime do here?
- Is any audio or transcript saved?
- What happens if the network or Bluetooth fails during a pitch?

## Product questions

- What's the difference between Telepath and the coaching agent?
- Who sends the cues, the teammate or the AI?
- How does the teammate send a cue without anyone noticing?
- Can the AI make things up? How do you stop that?
- What are the three coaching scenarios?
- Does the Fiber AI profile lookup work yet?

The last one is a trap. Fiber is planned, not built. A good answer says so.

## Hard follow-ups

Ask these right after the presenter answers one of the questions above.

- "You said it quotes sources. What if the source is wrong?"
- "How fast is the cue, really? Isn't three seconds too slow in a conversation?"
- "Why would someone wear AirPods during a pitch? Isn't that obvious to the judges?"
- "Isn't this just cheating in interviews?"
- "Why do you need a teammate if the AI can do it all?"

## Questions the docs don't cover yet

The coach should stay quiet on these or give a generic cue. Add a pitch doc that answers them before the real pitch.

- How is this different from Cluely?
- What's the business model? Who pays?
- How big is the market?
- What would you build next with another month?
- What was the hardest bug you hit this weekend?

## What to watch for

- **Relevance.** Did the cue fit the question, or was it a generic line like "Restate the question. Give one example."?
- **Timing.** Did it arrive before the presenter started talking, or too late to help?
- **Missed points.** After the answer, did the presenter hear a reminder of something they left out?
- **Honesty.** On the uncovered questions, did the coach stay quiet instead of making something up?
