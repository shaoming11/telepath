# Shao: interview stories and positions

Fictional test persona for the Telepath interview scenario. Each section is a ready story the coach can point to.

## Tell me about yourself

Shao builds real-time tools where speed matters. Two co-op terms on latency-sensitive systems, then hackathon projects with real users. The current focus is Telepath, an in-ear AI coach for live conversations.

## Why do you want this role

Shao wants to work where audio, networking and AI meet. The role fits because it ships user-facing real-time features, and Shao's best work at Lumen Audio was cutting latency from 220 ms to 90 ms.

## Biggest strength

Debugging under pressure. At Lumen Audio, Shao traced choppy audio to a polling loop by adding per-session packet-loss graphs, then replaced it with a WebSocket push pipeline.

## Biggest weakness

Shao used to over-build before talking to users. With StudyLoop, two weeks went into a feature nobody used. Now Shao ships a rough version in a few days and watches how people use it before polishing.

## A time you failed

At Northpine Logistics, a database migration Shao wrote took down staging for an hour. Shao rolled it back, wrote the postmortem, and added a dry-run step to the migration script that the team still uses.

## A time you disagreed with someone

At Lumen Audio, a senior engineer wanted to fix latency by buying faster servers. Shao argued the bottleneck was the polling loop, built a one-day prototype to prove it, and the team switched approaches after seeing the numbers.

## A time you led a team

For StudyLoop at Hack the North 2025, Shao split the four-person team by layer, ran 20-minute check-ins every three hours, and cut two features at midnight so the demo would work. The team placed second out of about 200.

## Hardest technical problem

Keeping AI cues fast enough to be useful in a live conversation. In Telepath, Shao starts drafting the answer while the question is still being asked, then only plays it once the question is complete.

## Where do you see yourself in five years

Leading a small team that builds real-time AI products, ideally something people use in live conversations.

## Questions Shao likes to ask interviewers

- What does the first 90 days look like for someone in this role?
- What is the hardest technical problem the team is working on right now?
- How do you decide when a feature is ready to ship?
- What do the best interns or new grads on your team do differently?

## Salary and logistics

- Looking for a summer 2027 internship or a new-grad role starting in 2028.
- Open to Toronto, Waterloo, San Francisco or New York.
- Expected internship pay: in line with the market for the city. Shao prefers to hear the company's range first.
