# The conversation you didn't hear

A room watches the pitch. A notices the judges. B notices A speaking faster. The pitch keeps going while they exchange a cue.

**Audience** Judges on a projector, listening to a presenter who needs both hands and their eyes on the room.

**One sentence** Your teammate can change how the pitch goes without taking the floor.

| Hold | On the projector | In the room | Product framing for the film |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | “I have one minute to explain this.” | A says the line, starts explaining the demo, then deliberately picks up the pace. B sits among the audience. Operator has already started the session. | Establish two separate listening positions, with generous empty space between them. |
| Send | “Slow down.” | B uses **Single** in the phone’s Live controls. A waits for the private chime and whisper, finishes the sentence, takes a breath and continues. Operator holds until that change is visible. | Isolate the coach’s stem and mark the single input. Use the light-blue accent for B. |
| Receipt | “I heard you.” | After playback begins, A acknowledges within the receipt window. B hears the return tone. This headline expresses the meaning of the receipt, not spoken dialogue. | Return to B. A’s amber identifies the source of the acknowledgement. |
| Private help | “Why does it need the Mac?” | A judge asks aloud. A arms Question Mode while the question is being asked, hears a private cue if available and answers in their own words. On a keyless run, explain or skip this beat. | Hold on A and clear the space around the question. No return receipt or seat-to-seat total for this private answer. |
| Approval | “Send this to your teammate?” | Explain a separate path. B hears a draft in the assistant voice. Only after the preview does B approve delivery to A. For a rehearsed sequence, use the simulated prompt. | Keep the frame on B during the preview. Introduce A only for approved delivery. |
| Wrap | “Wrap it up now.” | B uses Out. After A hears it, A finishes the pitch. The operator reveals the session and opens the projected ledger. | Resolve to both seats. Leave space for the live ledger. |
| Reveal | “Here’s what passed between us.” | Show the session’s real messages, playback confirmations and acknowledgements. Use the recorded-take banner if playing the bundled fallback. | End in real stage UI. Read counts from the hub. |

**Presenter control** The projected story is a sequence of holds. Advance manually after each observable action. The site can open the stage independently. Looking at a story beat never sends a message, starts a microphone, or changes session phase.

**Setup** Obtain consent, connect both seats, select voices and check the earbuds. Start the room microphone only when appropriate. In Pitch, the physical single callback can toggle Question Mode. Use the named phone control for the fixed Single demonstration. The fixed cues use cached audio. Dynamic answers depend on available providers. The operator keeps Canned take available.
