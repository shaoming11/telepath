## Three camera treatments

1. **Across the room — recommended.** Keep the two seats in stable positions. Cut between the coach’s tap, the presenter’s change of pace and the returning receipt. It makes an invisible exchange easy to follow from a projector. Keep enough negative space around each seat for the captions.
2. **Inside the gesture.** Follow one stem in macro, pull back as the cue lands and shift focus for the receipt. The detail of the acoustic mesh can carry the image. The product asset has to survive much closer inspection.
3. **The second conversation.** Stay with the presenter for the opening. At Reveal, replay the same interval from the coach’s side, using actual event times to pair the views. This rewards attention but asks the audience to remember the opening action.

All three fit the same Wasp, React Three Fiber and timestamp-driven capture contract. The selection affects shot construction, lighting and the complete film render.
